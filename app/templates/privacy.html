Privacy Policy

Effective 2 November 2026 · Last updated 3 November 2026

This policy explains what information Beyric Note Taker collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies to our website, web app and emails.

Who we are

Beyric Note Taker is operated by Beyrichq LTD ("Beyric", "we", "us"), Lagos, Nigeria. We are the data controller for account and usage information. For meeting content that our users record, we act on behalf of the user who sent the note taker, and that user decides what is recorded.

Questions about this policy or how we handle your information go to privacy@beyrictech.com.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name and email address, and a password (stored only in hashed form) or, if you sign in with Google, your Google account identifier. We also keep a count of the hours of meetings recorded for your account, and, if you join the waiting list, your email address.
  • Settings and content you create: notes templates and vocabulary terms.
  • Messages you send us, such as support requests.

Meeting content

When you send the note taker to a meeting, we collect:

  • Audio from the meeting and, where the platform provides it, the display names of participants and who was speaking.
  • The transcript produced from that audio.
  • Notes, summaries, decisions and action items produced from the transcript.
  • Meeting details such as the platform, meeting link or ID, date, time and duration.
  • Files you export (a PDF or Word file of your notes, and of the transcript if you choose it) are built when you ask and are not stored.

We do not record video or screen sharing.

Information collected automatically

  • Server logs, which include your IP address, the pages you request and the time of each request. We use them to run and secure the service.
  • One cookie that keeps you signed in, described under Cookies below.

Google user data

If you connect your Google account, we ask only for the access the features below need, and Google shows you each permission before you agree.

If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and Google account identifier, and nothing else, unless you also connect your calendar.

  • Google Calendar: we read the title, time, organiser, attendees and meeting link of your upcoming events. We use this only to decide which meetings to join, following the rules you choose, and to show your upcoming meetings in the app. We do not create, change or delete calendar events.
  • Google Meet: if you turn on joining meetings you start yourself, Google tells us when a Meet call you host begins, with its meeting code, so the note taker can join it. We do not receive the meeting's audio, video or chat from Google.

Beyric Note Taker's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • We use Google user data only to provide and improve the features described above, which you can see in the app.
  • We do not transfer Google user data to others except as needed to provide those features, for security, to comply with the law, or as part of a merger or sale with your notice.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not sell it.
  • We do not use Google user data to develop, improve or train generalised artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
  • People at Beyric do not read Google user data unless you ask us to for support, it is needed for security or to investigate abuse, or the law requires it.

You can disconnect your Google account at any time in the app or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. When you disconnect, we stop reading your calendar and delete the calendar data we hold.

How we use your information

  • To provide the service: joining meetings, recording audio, and producing transcripts and notes for you.
  • To apply your templates and vocabulary so notes follow your format and spell names correctly.
  • To send service emails, such as account verification, password resets, waiting-list invitations and notices that an address already has an account.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and fix problems.
  • To meet legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your meeting content for advertising. We do not use your meeting audio, transcripts or notes to train artificial intelligence models, and our providers do not use them for training either (see below).

Who we share information with

  • OpenAI turns meeting audio into a transcript and writes notes from it. Under OpenAI's API terms, data sent through its API is not used to train its models.
  • Resend sends our service emails (account verification, password resets, waiting-list invitations and notices that an address already has an account). It receives the email address and, if given, the name on each of those emails, and their content, which includes one-time links.
  • Contabo hosts our server, in France.
  • Cloudflare provides our domain's DNS and may carry traffic to the site.
  • Meeting and calendar platforms (Google and Zoom), as needed to join meetings and to read calendar events you have authorised.
  • Authorities, when required by law or court order, or to protect the rights, safety or property of Beyric, our users or others.
  • A buyer or successor, if Beyric is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

The software that joins meetings runs on our own server; no other company receives the recording from it.

Recording and participant notice

The note taker appears in the meeting's participant list while it records. Recording laws differ by country and state, and some require the consent of everyone in the conversation.

If you use Beyric Note Taker, you are responsible for telling participants that the meeting is being recorded and for getting any consent the law requires. Any participant can ask the host to remove the note taker, and recording stops when it leaves.

If you took part in a meeting recorded by one of our users and want a copy of, or changes to, information about you, contact that user first. You can also write to us and we will help.

How long we keep information

  • Meeting audio: deleted automatically {{ audio_retention_days }} days after the meeting.
  • Transcripts and notes: kept until you ask us to delete them or close your account.
  • Calendar data: we keep only what is needed for upcoming meetings, and delete it when you disconnect your calendar.
  • Account information: kept while your account is active and deleted within 30 days of closing it, except where the law requires us to keep records.
  • An account whose email address is not confirmed within 48 hours is deleted.
  • Waiting list: your email address is kept until you are invited and sign up, or you ask us to remove it.
  • Hours counted: when an account is deleted, we keep only the number of recorded hours counted for it, with a one-way fingerprint of the email address, so that signing up again does not reset the pilot's hours limit.
  • Backups: database backups are deleted after 14 days.

Security

Traffic to the site is encrypted with TLS. Access to the server is limited to the people who run it, the software that joins meetings runs on a separate network from our database, and secrets are kept out of our code. Our administrator can see account details (name, email, status and hours used) and, if you ask for help signing in, can create a one-time password reset link for your account, which would give access to it. You are notified in the app when this happens, and the action is logged. The administrator's account screens do not show your meetings or notes. No system is completely secure. If a personal data breach affects you, we will tell you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

International transfers

Our server is in France. OpenAI may process meeting audio and transcripts, and Resend the email addresses and names it sends mail to, in the United States. When personal data leaves Nigeria, the UK or the European Economic Area, we rely on the safeguards those laws allow, such as standard contractual clauses in our providers' terms.

Legal bases for processing

Where data protection laws such as the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, the GDPR or the UK GDPR apply, we rely on:

  • Performance of a contract, to provide the service you signed up for.
  • Legitimate interests, to secure and maintain the service, where those interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Consent, where we ask for it, such as when you connect your Google account. You can withdraw it at any time.
  • Legal obligation, where the law requires us to keep or disclose information.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, to receive a copy of it, to restrict or object to some processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority such as the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

To use any of these rights, email privacy@beyrictech.com. We will reply within 30 days and may need to confirm your identity first.

Children

Beyric Note Taker is not meant for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Cookies

We use one strictly necessary cookie to keep you signed in and to protect forms against forgery. We do not use analytics or advertising cookies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If we make material changes, we will tell you by email or in the app before they take effect. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact us

Beyrichq LTD, Lagos, Nigeria. Email: privacy@beyrictech.com.
