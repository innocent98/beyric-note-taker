Joins your calls

Send the note taker to a Google Meet or Zoom link. It records quietly and leaves when the meeting ends.

Notes in your format

Templates for client calls, interviews and team updates decide what the notes focus on. Write your own in plain language.

Names spelled right

Add the names and terms your team uses, and transcripts and notes spell them the way you do.

Coming soon: your calendar

Connect Google Calendar and the note taker joins the meetings you choose by itself, including Google Meet calls you start on the spot. It only reads what it needs to decide which meetings to join, and you can disconnect at any time. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what it reads.

How it works

  1. Paste a meeting link. Choose a template and send the note taker in.
  2. Have your meeting. Admit it from the waiting room and talk as usual. Tell everyone it is recording.
  3. Get the notes. Read or copy them minutes after the call ends.
