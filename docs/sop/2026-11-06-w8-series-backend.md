# SOP: W8 Series, backend (PR 1 of 2)

## What shipped
Recurring meetings ("series") for a team: meetings linked to a series from the calendar's recurring-event id (or by hand), the Recurring cards, the Series page payload (unfinished tasks, finished since the last meeting, carried open questions, agenda lines, past meetings, attendees), the agenda ("Other agenda items"), **Send agenda to attendees**, the **reminder mails** (1 hour and 30 minutes before) with their team switch and timing and a per-person preference, the meeting page's `series` and `open_questions` fields, and `PATCH /api/questions/{id}` (On next agenda, Mark resolved). Backend only: the screens are PR 2 (`feature/w8-series-screens`, stacked on this one). Migration **0030** (its `down_revision` is 0026: whoever merges after #96 (0027), #100 (0028) or #99 (0029) re-points it; `alembic heads` must show one head). Branch `feature/w8-series-backend`. PR: see the PR page (not merged).

## Why
Spec section 7, decisions D12, D13, D16, ambiguity rows 5, 7, 8. Meetings that repeat should remember each other: what is still open, what was asked and not answered, what the team wants to cover next, and a nudge before the call. The risky parts are mail (a person can make our domain write to teammates) and visibility (a series is shared with a team but its meetings are not), so each is one function with its own tests.

## How: the decisions
| Topic | Decision | Alternatives rejected |
|---|---|---|
| Where a series comes from | `series.link_from_calendar` runs when a calendar meeting's notes are written (`pipeline._step_notes`, in a savepoint, never failing the notes). It finds the event through `scheduled_joins.meeting_id` and creates or reuses the team's series for that `recurring_event_id`. An hourly sweep `series.sync_from_joins` makes series for events the scheduler will join (join not skipped/cancelled), so a card exists before the first recording. A person may also `POST /api/series` and `PUT /api/meetings/{id}/series` | Reading the recurring id at meeting creation (that is `join_runner.py`, which #96 rewires: not touched); a title regex (spec row 8); a series for every recurring event on the calendar (a recurring lunch would clutter the cards) |
| One series per team and recurring id | partial unique `(team_id, calendar_recurring_id)`: two teammates with the same event share one series | One per owner (duplicate cards for the same meeting) |
| Visibility | a series (title) belongs to the team (`series.team_id`); `GET` of another team's id is 404. **Everything inside is filtered per viewer**: meetings by `can_read_clause`, tasks by `tasks.visible`, questions through their meeting. Counts on the cards use the same filters, so two teammates see different counts | Counting everything and hiding the lists (a count leaks that a private meeting exists) |
| Cadence and next date | computed, never stored: `calendar_recurring.describe_cadence` over the stored event times of the team's members plus the start times of the series' joined meetings; next = the earliest stored future event. Nothing is fetched from Google. A hand-made series has no next date | A cadence column (goes stale) |
| Carried questions | `open_questions.carry` (default true) is "On next agenda"; carried = `carry` and not resolved, from series meetings the viewer can read, **one per wording** (newest meeting). Resolving resolves the same wording in the series' other meetings the caller may change | Carrying forever; a separate "carried" table |
| Who changes a question | the meeting's owner, or an owner/admin of the team the meeting is shared with; a member who can only read gets 403 `team_forbidden` | Any reader (a teammate could close the owner's question) |
| Agenda | plain text, 200 characters, **no web address**, 30 waiting lines per series, any member adds, author or manager removes. "Consumed" (`consumed_at`) when the pipeline links a recorded calendar meeting that began after the line was typed (a hand attach consumes nothing); `consumed_by_meeting_id` only says which and goes null if that meeting is deleted (the line stays consumed: `tests/test_meeting_delete.py` KEPT list) | Deleting consumed lines (the history is cheap) |
| Send agenda | recipients = **active members of the series' team** who spoke in (name matched like a task owner) or recorded one of the last three meetings, not the sender, with `notify_reminders` on, at most 10. **No typed outside address:** the designer drew one button, and outside mail needs the opt-out machinery that is in #99 (W6) | Typing addresses (a stranger could be mailed from our domain with no opt-out on main) |
| Mail kind | both mails are **notes templates**: the outbox row holds ids only (`{"send_id","user_id"}`, `{"reminder_id"}`), the text is built when the mail is sent, with its own unique index so once is once. They are counted in the daily and per-recipient notes caps and held by the hourly budget like other bulk mail | Link templates (their payload would hold text) |
| What a recipient sees | built **per recipient**: the last meeting's summary/decisions only if they can read that meeting; carried questions and unfinished tasks only as they can see them; agenda lines are team content. Tasks assigned to the recipient show even from a private meeting (spec 8.2, on purpose) | One mail for everyone (it would publish a private meeting to teammates who attended) |
| Mail safety | all text through `titles.mail_line` (URLs become "[link removed]", domains get `[.]`), `clean_line` for the subject/title (no line breaks), the layout's escaped blocks; the only links are our own `/series/{id}` and the event's meeting link, **only if https on meet.google.com or zoom.us** | Passing the event link through unchecked |
| Send-time checks | recipient still active, address unchanged, still on the team, preference on; sender still active and on the team; team reminders still on and the kind still wanted; occurrence not moved (`starts_at` differs from the event row), not cancelled, not started | Trusting what was true when queued |
| Reminders | off by default (`teams.reminders_on` false; the Google-data wording in the policy must land first, spec 7). A job every minute on the sync loop (`series_reminders`) looks at stored events of series whose team has reminders on: the **1-hour mail goes from 60 to 35 minutes before, the 30-minute mail from 30 to 5**. A scheduler that was down misses a mail rather than send it late. `series_reminders` has a unique `(series, user, kind, starts_at)`, so once per person and occurrence. A series with no past meeting and no agenda sends nothing | A single 90-minute mail (the designer drew two) |
| Abuse caps (agenda) | 10 people a send, 5 sends and 20 people per sender per rolling day, one at a time per sender (row lock); 409 `no_attendees`, 409 `nothing_to_send`, 429 `agenda_rate_limited` | Caps only at the mail sender (the user would not learn why) |
| Team changes | joining a team moves the series (a twin with the same recurring id is merged: meetings and agenda lines move, the duplicate goes); leaving a team takes the leaver's meetings out of that team's series. `meeting.series` shows only when the series belongs to the viewer's team | Leaving a pointer into a team that can no longer read the meeting |
| `series.project_id` | plain column, no foreign key: projects (W4, #100) arrive in their own migration. W4 writes it and `filing.series_project` reads it | An FK to a table that may not exist yet |

## Opus review (2026-11-06) and what changed
No Critical. Two High, fixed with tests: (1) the series title and names reached mail with live web addresses (now `titles.mail_line`, domains defanged); (2) a teammate's **private recurring call was published as a team card** (now a series is shown only if made by hand, made by the viewer, has a meeting the viewer can read, or is on the viewer's own calendar; `_timing` reads only readable meetings). Mediums fixed: a hand attach no longer consumes agenda lines (only a recorded calendar meeting does); "Send agenda" names only people from meetings the sender can read. Not fixed, noted: a stranger's recurring invites can fill the 100-series cap through `link_from_calendar`; any member's event decides reminder `starts_at` for a shared recurring id; `sync_from_joins` takes 200 groups with no cursor; merging a twin series drops its reminder rows (a second reminder is possible once).

## Operate / roll back
- No new environment variable, no nginx line, no cron. The reminder job is on the existing scheduler; **reminders are off for every team until an owner/admin switches them on** (PR 2 Settings card, or `PATCH /api/team`).
- Roll back: `alembic downgrade 0026` drops the new tables and columns (series, agenda, flags); mail rows already queued for `series_agenda`/`series_reminder` would fail with "unknown template" once the code is reverted, which is harmless.
