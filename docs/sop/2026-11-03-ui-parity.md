# SOP: Next.js frontend brought to the designer's files (UI parity)

## What shipped
The pages in `web/` now follow the designer's files in `docs/ui/Beyric Note Taker redesign/` (as merged in PR #23): the new "b" logo and brand tokens, the app shell, Meetings, the meeting page, Templates, Vocabulary, the sign-in screen, the landing page, the privacy and terms pages, the 404 and error pages, the favicon, the PWA and Apple icons and a share image. Branch `feature/nextjs-ui-parity`, PR #26, merged and deployed 2026-11-03 (CI green; deploy 05:54 UTC). The screen-by-screen account is `docs/ui/parity-report.md`.

## Why
Adebayo opened https://notes.beyrictech.com after the Next.js front door (PR #19) and the owner screens (PR #21) went live and said it was not in parity with the designer's handoff. The screens had been built from the prototype's values, but the designer then updated Auth, Landing, Brand, Emails and the logo (PR #23), the logo and landing were never ported, and a gap check had not been run page against page. This stream ran that check at 1440, 768 and 390 px and fixed every difference on the screens that exist.

## How
| Piece | Decision |
|---|---|
| Source of truth | The newest files win (`Auth`, `Landing`, `Brand`); `Auth v1` and `Landing v1` are reference only. `Brand.dc.html` is the token source |
| Tokens (`web/styles/tokens.css`) | Every colour the designs use is a named token (inks `--ink-2` and `--ink-3`, soft borders, washes, the avatar pairs, the landing night palette, status pairs from the prototype's `ST` map). Nothing is a one-off override, and the guard test (no colour literal outside the tokens file) still passes. The designs are **light only**, so the dark overrides stream A had added were removed, with `BRAND.bgDark` and the dark theme-colour. The Jinja pages keep their own `app/static/tokens.css` (not touched: `app/` is outside this change), so the two files differ until the Jinja pages are gone |
| Logo and icons | One `LogoMark` SVG (the b stroke and the dot, viewBox 120) used by `Logo` in three tile sizes. The favicon (`app/icon.svg`) is the 16 px variant Brand describes. `scripts/make-icons.sh` copies the designer's PNG exports to `public/brand/`, resizes the 1024 export for `icon-192` and `icon-512`, draws the full-bleed maskable and Apple icons from the same geometry, and builds `public/og.png` from the wide logo. Open Graph and Twitter large-card metadata is set in the layout and passed again by pages that set their own `openGraph` |
| Components | New props instead of new one-off CSS: `Button` sizes `md` (36 px), `field` (sign-in), `xl` (landing) and kinds `quiet`, `outline-light`; `Field` `large` and `end` (a control inside the right edge); `SelectField` `compact`; `Card` `compact`; `Badge` `small`; `Alert` draws the designer's icons; `Logo` `size` and `tone`; `Icon` `stroke`. All shown in `/styleguide` |
| Sign in | `components/site/auth-shell.tsx` (form left, teal panel with the product preview right, as the prototype's grid) and a password-only form. The email field, Google and Microsoft, forgot password, keep-me-signed-in and create account are not drawn: no endpoint (listed in the report) |
| Landing | `components/landing/landing.tsx`, section by section from the prototype; words and icon paths in `content.ts`. The FAQ is native `<details name="faq">`, so it works without JavaScript and keeps the exclusive-open behaviour. Sign-up buttons and "See the app" are not drawn. The page `<title>`, description, JSON-LD and `llms.txt` stay as they were (true statements only) |
| Legal pages | `components/site/legal-shell.tsx` gives the prototype's frame (header tabs, sticky "On this page", footer). The text stays the live legal text, so `tests/legal.test.tsx` still compares it with the Jinja templates; `components/legal/toc.ts` lists the headings and `tests/parity.test.tsx` fails if the list drifts |
| Notes on the meeting page | `notes_html` is one flat HTML string. `wrapSections` splits it at each `<h2>` and wraps the part in a `<section>` whose class comes from a fixed list (summary, key points, decisions, action items, open questions, please check), so CSS can draw check marks for decisions, quiet dots for open questions and the grey "Please check" box. Nothing from the notes goes into an attribute (tested with a hostile heading) |
| Responsive | Landing, sign-in and legal follow the prototype's own grids. The prototype's main app is not responsive (it crushes at 390 px), so the phone layout from stream C stays, with the new tokens. The landing header hides its section links below 720 px (the prototype's wrap and clip). `overflow-wrap: anywhere` (the owner app's base rule) is switched off on the public layouts because it crushed flex items to a letter wide |
| Left alone on purpose | Live legal wording and entity (Beyrichq LTD), the `privacy@` mailbox, "Meeting link" placeholder without Teams, the API's status words. Listed with reasons in the report |
| Rejected | Copying the prototype's inline styles (the CSP and the guard test forbid them); keeping a dark theme the designer never drew; building the sign-up, forgot-password, calendar, team and task screens as pages without a backend |

## Operate / roll back
Nothing to configure. Deploy as usual (rebuild the `next` image); not while a meeting is `bot_sent` or `processing`. To go back, revert the merge commit: the previous pages come back as they were. `web/scripts/parity-shots.mjs` repeats the screenshots (usage in its header); `scripts/mock-api.mjs` serves the owner screens without a database.
