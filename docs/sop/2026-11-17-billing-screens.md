# SOP: billing stage S8b, the billing screens

**What shipped.** The screens for the money path S8a built: choose a billing country and a plan, pay on the provider's page, come back to a page that waits for the verified payment, see and manage the plan (cancel, resume, change, extra hours, history), and be told on every screen when a payment failed or the plan lapsed. PR from `feature/billing-screens` (built on top of S8a, PR #227, `feature/subscriptions-and-lapse`). Stage S8 of `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-15-billing-and-plans-design.md`. **No backend change.** Nothing here is on for anyone: every billing route answers 404 until `BILLING_ENABLED` and the allow-list say otherwise, and the screens treat that 404 as "not available" and draw nothing (or say so plainly on the page itself).

## Why

S8a made it possible to buy, but only through the API and a script. S8b is what lets a person do it, and what lets Adebayo run the test-mode purchase of the S8a runbook from a browser. There is **no billing design** in `docs/ui` (the only settings designs are the multi-team ones), so every screen is built from the existing components and Settings patterns and invents no visual language; the designer restyles it later. The words are the product: each consequence the FE guide lists under "What the person must be told" is written where the button is.

## How, and what was decided

| Screen | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Plans and checkout** (A) | `/billing/plans` | The billing country card (a select of the server's own country list; as it changes it reads `GET /billing/route` and states **"Prices in naira, paid through Paystack."** or **"Prices in US dollars, paid through Flutterwave."** before anything is saved or paid; the rules are said up front: where you are established, not guessed; a wrong first choice fixed once; one change in 30 days; a change between Nigeria and elsewhere with a live plan waits for the month's end and cannot be undone; a card from any country is accepted). Then the plans from `GET /plans` with the API's price (a plan with `price: null` says it is not on sale in that currency and is **listed, never hidden**), the "Before you pay" list (renews by card, cancel keeps the month, no refund of the unused part, no rollover, a price change applies at renewal, hours come when the provider confirms), and **Choose this plan** → `POST /checkout` → the browser leaves for `checkout_url`. A person who already has a plan is sent to Billing; a checkout started and not finished is offered back on its own page |
| **Return page** (A) | `/billing/return` | Reads **our** reference from `reference`, `trxref` or `tx_ref` (Paystack adds the first two; Flutterwave's names are not verified, so the third is read too) and falls back to the reference this tab stored in `sessionStorage` before leaving. Polls `GET /payments/{reference}` every 2 s for 30 s, then every 10 s to 5 min, stops on any state but `pending`. **"Payment received" is shown only for `succeeded`**; the address is never trusted (a `status=successful` in it changes nothing). Words for: confirming, paid (by kind), did not go through (no hours were added, try again), backed out (provider's `status=cancelled`: no hours, keep the page open, go back to the payment page), still confirming after 5 min (leave, hours appear when confirmed, keep the reference), refunded, no such payment (a 404 `not_found` on the payment while the state still answers: the same code also means billing is off, so the page asks the state), the connection lost for the whole 5 minutes (it says it stopped and offers **Check again**), no payment named, billing not available. One live region stays on the page and focus moves to the new answer |
| **Billing page** (B) | `/billing`, from a **Plan and billing** card on `/settings` | Plan card (status badge, renewal date and next charge; **a changed price is said**: "not the $X you paid last time"; cancel asks first and says access to the period's end, no refund of the unused part, extra hours stay, can be taken back; a cancelled plan says it ends and offers **Keep my plan**; the grace says no new hours until paid, the date, the next retry, and offers **Pay now**). Hours card (hours and minutes, never a decimal: "12 minutes", "1 hour 30 minutes"; left and used with the progress bar the Tasks milestones draw; the 80% and 100% sentences follow `hours.warning`, never the bar; what is closed without a plan). **Change plan** (only when `can_change_plan`): "See what changes" calls the preview, an upgrade says **"You pay $15.99 today"** and the extra hours and that the charge is immediate, a downgrade says nothing is charged now and when it starts, a waiting downgrade can be taken back. **Extra hours** (only with a live plan, a plan in its grace counts). Billing country (same card as A). **Payments** with the words Paid, Waiting, Did not go through, Refunded, Partly refunded and a waiting payment's own page |
| **App-wide notice** (C) | `BillingBanner` in `AppShell`, every owner screen except `/billing*` | `past_due`: "Your plan payment did not go through. No new hours are added until it is paid. Your plan ends on <date> if it stays unpaid. You can still record from the hours you have left." with **Pay now** (`POST /subscription/pay-link`). A charge the bank wants confirmed (`pending_payment.kind` `renewal` with a `pay_url`) shows **Finish paying** that goes to **that charge's own page**; the screen never asks for a second checkout. `lapsed`: what is closed, what stays, **Choose a plan** (and the bank's page if a charge still waits, since paying it late brings the plan back). Reads again every 60 s while shown and on focus. A healthy answer is remembered for a minute per tab (a notice is never remembered) |
| **Gates** (C) | `GateText` in the Ask card, the meeting page (rewrite and translate), the share dialog and page, new meeting, Not recorded | A 403 `plan_inactive` sentence ("... needs an active plan or trial ...") gets **See plans**, an `hours_cap_reached` sentence ("... will not join new meetings ...") gets **Add hours or choose a plan** (Billing). The server's sentence is shown as it comes. A `reserve` refusal is a wait and gets no link. **Ask no longer offers "Try again" on `plan_inactive`** (it is not transient; before, it read as "Beyric could not answer that") |

**Updated 2026-11-17 by `docs/sop/2026-11-17-billing-country-flow.md`:** the billing country card no longer asks cold or says "not guessed": it suggests a country from the saved time zone (or the browser language) for the person to confirm, the country changes freely until the first payment, and the 30-day rule ("a wrong first choice fixed once") now applies only from the first payment.

Decisions and rejected alternatives:

* **Three small pages, not one.** `/billing/plans` (buy), `/billing` (manage), `/billing/return` (wait). A single adaptive page was rejected: the return address is fixed by the server (`/billing/return`), and "choose" and "manage" answer different questions.
* **The data is read in the browser**, not on the server. A 404 from a billing route means "not available to this person", which must be a plain message, not the not-found page that the server helper `load()` turns every 404 into.
* **The banner is a client component in the shell**, because `AppShell` is a plain component rendered by about 25 pages (and by `renderToStaticMarkup` in tests): an async server child would break them, and passing a prop through every page was rejected. Cost: one `GET /api/billing/subscription` per page load for an allowed account, at most one a minute while all is well (the tab remembers a healthy answer for 60 s; a notice is never remembered). For everyone else the 404 is **remembered for the tab** (`sessionStorage`), so while billing is dark it is asked once per tab, not once per page. Not asked at all on the billing pages.
* **Words for the states the API does not have.** The API's payment statuses are `pending`, `succeeded`, `failed`, `refunded`, `partly_refunded`. "Abandoned" is only the provider's `status=cancelled` in the return address (a hint for the words; the payment is still read from us and a late success still shows). "Mismatch" (a wrong amount, currency or reference: the server refuses it and the payment stays `pending`) cannot be told from a payment still being confirmed, so after 5 minutes it reads "still confirming ... keep the reference and tell us". See **contract gaps**.
* **No price in code.** Every number comes from `GET /plans`, the subscription, a preview or a payment. `/pricing` is untouched (S11 prints numbers).
* **Country list** is a copy of the server's `ISO_COUNTRIES` (`web/lib/countries.ts`, a test compares it with `app/billing/countries.py`); names come from `Intl.DisplayNames`, so no name is typed.
* **Billing routes are not in `docs/openapi.json`**, so their types are written by hand in `web/lib/billing.ts` from the guide's captures (the generated `schema.d.ts` has none).

## Money review round (Opus review of #229, `review-pr229-money.md`)

Fixed: the upgrade confirm could be pressed twice (the server does not de-duplicate upgrades; a second paid upgrade becomes a refund by hand); the cancel question in the grace (access runs to the grace's end, a cancel in the grace cannot be taken back: it now says so); the success page no longer claims "your plan is active and your hours are added" (a `succeeded` payment can still have granted nothing, as in a double payment: it says "Your plan and hours are shown on Billing"); a second, different plan page cannot be started without a warning and a second yes while one waits; the screens refuse to redirect when the payment the server made differs from the price shown (plan, pack, upgrade), say the new price and re-read; the lapsed card and banner say "do one of the two, not both" when a renewal charge still waits; "Finish paying" (not "Confirm with your bank") for any waiting renewal page; no end date for a lapsed or ended plan (the screen does not know the grace end of an ended plan, and the period end was three days early); "Try again" after a failed pack, upgrade or renewal goes to Billing; the lapse copy says calendar auto-join follows the hours; keeping a cancelled plan says what will be charged and when.

**Wording that is a promise: needs Adebayo's or the lawyer's confirmation before real money** (softened here, nothing invented): (1) "You can still read, search and export your notes" after a lapse (spec 3.6 retention; the old "all still here" / "stay as they are" is gone from the screens, the server's own out-of-hours sentence still says "stay as they are"); (2) "If our price changes, it applies from your next renewal" (the promise that the billing page shows it first was removed: it is a notice commitment against the "no notice of policy changes" decision); (3) "Cancelling does not refund the unused part of the month; payments are refunded only under our refund rules" (spec 3.5 has a 7-day first-plan refund, a 14-day pack refund and a refund when charged twice: the screens do not list the windows, and "our refund rules" must exist in the Terms, S11); (4) "You can pay with a card from another country, but your bank may decline it" (replaces "a card from any country is accepted").

## Contract gaps found (S8a's API; none worked around silently)

1. **No distinct state for a refused payment or an abandoned checkout.** A wrong amount, currency or reference leaves the payment `pending` forever, and an abandoned checkout is `pending` too; the screen can only say "still confirming, keep the reference". A `status` (or a `refused` reason) on the payment would let the page say "this payment did not match, you were not credited, we will look at it".
2. **The card's brand and last four digits** are null (S8a's own follow-up), so the plan card cannot say which card renews.
3. **Billing routes are missing from `docs/openapi.json`**, so the TypeScript types are hand-written and can drift.
4. **No way to tell "billing is on for me" without a billing call.** The session has `usage.state` only under `HOURS_MODE=enforce`. A boolean in `GET /api/session` would let the shell skip the banner's request for everyone not allowed.
5. **No contact address for "tell us".** The return page asks the person to keep the payment reference and "tell us", with no address (the old `hello@` sentence was removed on purpose; `privacy@` is the only confirmed mailbox). Adebayo should name a support address before real money.

## Operate / roll back

Nothing to run: the pages ship with the web container. They are inert until `BILLING_ENABLED` is on for an account. To try them locally: `docker run -d --name bn-s8b-pg -e POSTGRES_PASSWORD=scratch -p 55432:5432 postgres:16-alpine`, `python scripts/dev_billing_stack.py postgresql+psycopg://postgres:scratch@localhost:55432/postgres`, then in `web/`: `FASTAPI_INTERNAL_URL=http://127.0.0.1:8765 npx next dev -p 3100`; sign in as `new@example.test` ... (the script prints the password; `GET /dev/` lists the accounts and `GET /dev/scenario/<name>` builds each state). Roll back: revert the PR; the API is untouched.
