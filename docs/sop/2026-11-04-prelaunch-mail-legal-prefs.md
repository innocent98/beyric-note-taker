# SOP: before sign-up opens, part 1: legal wording, email preferences, skipped-mail notice

## What shipped
Three of the follow-ups the W0.3 mail upgrade left "before sign-up opens for anyone but the owner": (a) the privacy and terms "Last updated" date and `TERMS_VERSION` moved to 4 November 2026, and the two sentences that promised notice of changes now say the texts may change from time to time (**no notice of any kind is sent when the policy changes**, Adebayo's decision 2026-11-04); (b) an Email preferences switch (API, Settings screen, link in the notes-ready mail); (c) a notes mail skipped for the per-recipient cap now shows up as a warning notification to the meeting's owner. Branch `feature/prelaunch-mail-legal-prefs`, one PR (number in the PR list). No migration, no new environment variable. Part 2 (flood defence, mail budget, database-backed counters) and part 3 (token links as `#fragment`) are separate PRs.

## Also in this PR: the default sender
`MAIL_FROM` defaulted to `no-reply@beyrictech.com` (`app/config.py`, `.env.example`); the domain Resend has verified, and the production `.env`, use `Beyric Note Taker <no-reply@notes.beyrictech.com>`. The default and `.env.example` now say that, `deploy/README.md` names it, and `tests/test_mail.py` pins it. The server `.env` is untouched.

## Why
W0.3 changed what the privacy text says (notes-ready mail, what Resend receives, the 30-day mail log, the do-not-mail list). The recorded version must follow the text an account accepted, the mail is on for everyone with no off switch, and a mail over the cap vanished silently. All of it must be fixed before anyone but the owner has an account.

## Operate / roll back
- **Server steps before merge: none.** No environment variable, no migration, no nginx change.
- Roll back: revert the PR. A skipped-mail notification already written stays (a harmless row; `delete from notifications where kind = 'mail.recipient_cap'` removes them). `TERMS_VERSION` and the legal sentences go back with the revert; accounts made in between keep the newer version value.
