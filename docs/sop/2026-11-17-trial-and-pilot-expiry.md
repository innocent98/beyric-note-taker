# SOP: billing stage S10, the one-time trial and the end of the pilot hours

**What shipped.** (1) A one-time **trial**: a `trial` hour grant of `TRIAL_SECONDS` (3 hours = 10800) given once per person when their email is first confirmed (password link or Google), ending at the hours used or 14 days after it is given, whichever is first. It also closes the S2 "before enforce" gap: accounts made after migration 0049 hold no hours, and a scheduler sweep gives every such account its trial. (2) The **pilot hours' end**: with `PAID_PLANS_OPEN_ON` set, every pilot grant ends 60 days later, and each person with pilot hours left gets one mail and one bell entry 14 days before. (3) A **trial budget** (`TRIAL_DAILY_BUDGET_SECONDS`) on how many trial seconds can start in any 24 hours; `max_accounts` stays as the other brake. **All of it is dark**: `TRIAL_SECONDS` defaults to 0, `PAID_PLANS_OPEN_ON` is unset, and both only act while the hours rule is in force (`HOURS_MODE=enforce` or `FEATURE_GATES_MODE=enforce`); with those off no row is written for anyone and no extra scheduler job runs. PR: see the checklist line under S10; branch `feature/trial-and-pilot-expiry`. It is stage S10 of `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-15-billing-and-plans-design.md` (sections 3.8, 3.9, 8, 11). **No migration** (the migration number 0053 stays free for S9). FE guide: `docs/fe-integration-guide-billing.md` (section "The trial and the pilot's end"; captures in `docs/fe-captures/trial/`).

## Why

Under `HOURS_MODE=enforce` a person is allowed to record only from hours they hold. An account made after migration 0049 holds none, so every new sign-up would be refused `no_plan` (S2 SOP, "Before switching on", point 1). The trial is the way a new person gets hours without a card. The pilot hours had no end, so a pilot account that only reads would keep Ask, translation and rewriting switched on for ever (spec 3.9); Adebayo decided on 2026-11-16 that they expire 60 days after paid plans open, with one mail before.

## How, and what was decided

| Piece | What it does |
|---|---|
| **The trial grant** (`app/trial.py`) | `give` writes a `kind='trial'` grant: `seconds = TRIAL_SECONDS`, `starts_at` = the moment it is given, `expires_at` = that plus `TRIAL_DAYS`, `email_hash` = the fingerprint of the address. Everything after that is the existing rule: spent first (spec 3.2), exactly 3 hours used gives `no_hours`, the 14th day gives `none`/`no_plan`, the 80% and 100% notices work as for any grant |
| **Once per person and per fingerprint** | No trial for an admin, an unconfirmed, suspended or deleted account, or anyone (found by user id **or** email fingerprint, `entitlements.mine`) who has ever held a `trial` **or `pilot`** grant, ended or not. A deleted account's grants keep their fingerprint (the user id is set null, `app/deletion.py`), so deleting and signing up again with the same address gives nothing; a pilot account has its own hours, even when all are used. An app-wide advisory lock (6021) serialises the check and the insert, so two workers cannot give one person two trials or spend one budget twice |
| **When it is given** | At the three places an account becomes confirmed: `signup.verify_email` (password link), `google_signin._create` (a new Google account) and `google_signin._link_by_email` (Google confirming an unconfirmed password account). It runs in that transaction and in a savepoint: a failure is logged by exception class and never fails the sign-up. The settings reach it from the two routes (`api_accounts.py`) |
| **The sweep** (`trial.sweep`, scheduler job `trial_sweep`, every minute, registered only while `TRIAL_SECONDS` > 0) | Gives a trial to every confirmed, active, non-admin account that holds no trial or pilot grant, oldest confirmation first. It is the safety net for a failed sign-up hook, the way budget-delayed people get theirs, and **the fix for the 0049 gap**: when the flag goes on, everyone who made an account after 0049 gets a trial within a minute. A late trial **starts when it is given**: the 14 days are never spent waiting |
| **Only while the hours rule is in force** | `trial.enabled` = `TRIAL_SECONDS` > 0 **and** (`HOURS_MODE=enforce` or `FEATURE_GATES_MODE=enforce`). Decided: under `off` and `shadow` the old cap decides and nothing allocates recordings to grants, so a trial given then would be spent without ever being counted against it; it would be a second, unspent 3 hours on the day `enforce` goes on. So with the flags off the whole feature writes nothing (tested), and `TRIAL_SECONDS` can be set early without effect |
| **The budget** | `TRIAL_DAILY_BUDGET_SECONDS` (0 = no cap): a trial is given only if the trial seconds given in the last 24 hours (a rolling window, `starts_at > now - 24h`) plus one more still fit. Exactly the budget is allowed; a grant 24 hours old is out of the window. People past the budget hold nothing until the sweep gives them theirs. A budget smaller than one trial would stop every trial silently, so it stops the process at startup. Rolling, not a calendar day: no midnight cliff and no time-zone argument |
| **`max_accounts`** | Unchanged (`app/signup.py`): a sign-up past it never becomes an account, so it never reaches a trial. Tested with the trial on |
| **The pilot end** (`app/pilot_expiry.py`, job `pilot_expiry`, hourly, registered only while `PAID_PLANS_OPEN_ON` is set) | Unset: nothing happens. Set: every pilot grant still in force gets `expires_at` = the start of the day after `PAID_PLANS_OPEN_ON + PILOT_EXPIRY_DAYS` (60), UTC. After that instant the grant is simply not in force, so `entitlements` (state, join, gates) does the rest even if the scheduler is down. A grant already past its own end is never brought back by moving the date |
| **The one mail** | From `PILOT_EXPIRY_NOTICE_DAYS` (14) days before the end, each non-admin active person who holds pilot hours that are not used up gets one bell entry and one mail (`alerts.notify_user`, so `notify_email` and suppression are respected). The dedupe key names the end date: once per person **per date**. Moving the date sends one corrected mail for the new date, never two for one date |
| **Safety guard** | If the end is already past when the date is first seen and no mail was ever sent for it, nothing is expired and a warning is logged (`pilot expiry date ... has passed and no notice was ever sent`): nobody loses hours unwarned. Move the date |
| **Dry run** (`scripts/trial_report.py`) | Read-only; counts and dates only: who would get a trial now, how long a budget would take to clear them, who would be mailed and from what day |

Rejected: a trial in `off`/`shadow` (double hours at switch-on, above); an `app_state` column for the open date (it needs a migration that would collide with S8a's 0052 and S9's 0053, and Adebayo already edits `.env` for the other flags); expiring every pilot grant at one fixed instant without a mail window; a calendar-day budget (midnight cliff); a trial for admins; expiring pilot hours by a scheduled job at the end moment (the end is data on the grant, so it holds even with the scheduler down); counting `TRIAL_DAILY_BUDGET_SECONDS` on hours used rather than hours given (it would refuse a join mid-day because of strangers' use).

### The mail, word for word (a lawyer item: spec 3.9 and section 9 question 10)

Title: `Your pilot hours will expire`

> Paid plans opened on 1 February 2027. The recorded hours you still have from the pilot (1.5 h) can be used until the end of 2 April 2027 (UTC), 60 days later, and then they expire. After that your notes, transcripts and exports stay exactly as they are and you can still read, search and export them, but the note taker will not join new meetings, and Ask Beyric, new translations and rewriting notes with another template will not work, until you take a plan. Nothing is deleted. If you do not need more hours, you do not need to do anything.

The dates and the hours are filled in per person (here: `PAID_PLANS_OPEN_ON=2027-02-01`, 1.5 h left); the text is tested word for word in `tests/test_pilot_expiry.py`. **Caveat for the lawyer:** it changes what the pilot was promised ("5 hours for the pilot", the Terms line `web/components/legal/terms-content.tsx:30`). The wording, the 60 days and the date are theirs to confirm. **Do not set `PAID_PLANS_OPEN_ON` until they have.** The mail does not say "free" and does not name a price; it says "take a plan" and links nowhere because no plan can be bought before S8.

## Switch-on steps (for Adebayo; the deploy does none of this)

This **updates the S2 "Before switching on" list** (`docs/sop/2026-11-16-hours-enforce.md`): its point 1, the sign-up gap, is now closed by the trial.

1. **Deploy with all new settings unset.** Nothing changes: no row is written, no job registered. S9 and S8a may land before or after.
2. **Decide the numbers.** `TRIAL_SECONDS` (10800 = 3 hours, decided), `TRIAL_DAYS` (14, decided), `TRIAL_DAILY_BUDGET_SECONDS`: `0` (no cap) while `max_accounts` is the brake; if you want a cost cap too, a multiple of 10800 (43200 = 4 new trials in any 24 hours).
3. **Run the dry run** with the values you intend: `docker compose exec -T web python scripts/trial_report.py </dev/null` (it reads the settings the process has; to try values without editing `.env`, add `-e TRIAL_SECONDS=10800 -e HOURS_MODE=enforce` to the `exec`). It prints how many accounts would get a trial at the switch-on (the accounts made after 0049) and how many days a budget would take.
4. **The S2 steps as written, in order:** `HOURS_MODE=shadow`; `scripts/hours_reconcile_backlog.py` (dry run, read every `HOURS CHANGE`, then `--apply`); `scripts/hours_shadow_report.py`; the reconcile script again right before step 5. The accounts made after 0049 show as "old rule allows, new rule refuses: no grant in force" in that report: **that is expected now, and step 5 is what fixes it.**
5. **Switch the hours rule and the trial on in one edit of `.env`:** `HOURS_MODE=enforce`, `TRIAL_SECONDS=10800` (and the budget if you chose one), recreate web, worker and scheduler. Within a minute the scheduler's `trial_sweep` gives each account without a trial or pilot grant its 3 hours (check the log line `trial sweep gave N trial(s)`; N matches step 3 unless a budget spreads them over days). New sign-ups get theirs at once, when they confirm their email. Then run `scripts/hours_shadow_report.py` again: no disagreement should be left from the gap.
6. **Gates last:** `FEATURE_GATES_MODE=enforce` only at go-live (S11), **after** `HOURS_MODE=enforce` has been on, so a person's trial is counted from the first recording.
7. **The pilot end, later, and only after the lawyer has read the mail text above.** Run `scripts/hours_reconcile_backlog.py` (dry run, then `--apply`) once more first: it turns any usage counted in `shadow` into allocations so nothing comes off paid hours after the pilot ends. Run `scripts/trial_report.py` (it shows the end date, the first mail day and how many people would be mailed). Set `PAID_PLANS_OPEN_ON=<the day paid plans open>`, recreate the **scheduler**. The first run gives the pilot grants their end; the mail goes 14 days before it; read the log line `pilot expiry: N grant(s) given an end, M person(s) told`.

## Operate / roll back

* **Stop giving trials:** unset `TRIAL_SECONDS` (or `0`) and recreate web, worker and scheduler. Trials already given stay as data and keep working until they end; nothing is taken back.
* **Take back one trial:** a direct SQL edit of that `hour_grants` row (set its `seconds` to 0 or its `expires_at` to now); there is no screen and an admin gift only adds hours. Not built here.
* **Cancel the pilot end:** unsetting `PAID_PLANS_OPEN_ON` stops the job but **does not clear the end already written on the grants**. To clear it: `docker compose exec -T db psql -U postgres -d notetaker -c "update hour_grants set expires_at = null where kind = 'pilot' and expires_at > now()"` (only grants still in force; an ended grant stays ended). To move the date instead, set the new date: the grants are moved to it, and a corrected mail goes for the new date.
* **A person says they got no trial:** they are in `trial.waiting` if they are confirmed, active, not an admin and hold no trial or pilot grant; the sweep gives it within a minute unless the daily budget is spent (`scripts/trial_report.py` shows the free room). If they ever held a trial or a pilot grant under that address, they are not owed one.
* Revert the PR to remove the code; there is no migration to undo. Grants and notices already written stay.

## Follow-ups

| Item | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| The session payload has **no field for the pilot's end** (`pilot_ends_at`); only the mail and bell carry the date | the sidebar cannot show "your pilot hours end on ..." (an additive `Status` and `UsageOut` field, which S8a is editing: left out here on purpose) |
| The trial is only given while `HOURS_MODE` or `FEATURE_GATES_MODE` is `enforce`. With `FEATURE_GATES_MODE=enforce` and `HOURS_MODE` still `off`, a trial is given but recordings are not allocated to it, so that person could record up to the old cap and still hold 3 hours when `HOURS_MODE=enforce` follows | go live with the hours rule first (step 6 above), as the spec's order already says |
| A trial is not stopped by anything but the grant: no IP, device or card check; a person with many email addresses gets many trials. **Aliases count as different addresses**: `ada+1@gmail.com` and `a.da@gmail.com` have different fingerprints from `ada@gmail.com` (the fingerprint is `usage.email_hash`, the lower-cased trimmed address, as the ledger and spec 3.8 define it) | the brakes are a confirmed mailbox, `max_accounts`, the budget; a new trial costs about 2 to 3 USD (spec 3.8). Merging aliases (strip `+tag`, Gmail dots) would be a product decision that also changes what the ledger treats as one person, so it is **not done here**; raised by the automated security review of this commit, 2026-11-17. If trials start being farmed, add a canonical fingerprint column and a per-origin limit |
| Pilot hours end at the end of a UTC day, and the mail says so; a person in another zone sees the date in UTC | a per-person zone is stored (`#82`); using it in the mail is a small change if wanted |
| A trial given while the person is **not** a pilot but a reader-only teammate costs nothing unless they record; they still get one | spec 3.8 gives it to everyone at first confirmed sign-in |
| `trial.waiting` loads every waiting account each minute and the sweep stops at the first refused one | cheap for hundreds of accounts; add a `LIMIT` if sign-up ever floods |
| No admin screen for a trial or the pilot date; both are `.env` settings | Adebayo edits `.env` for the other flags; an `app_state` column would need a migration |
| Google sign-in, the 14th day, the budget window and delete-and-re-register are not captured over HTTP | see "Not verified" |
| `scripts/capture_trial.py` rides on `scripts/capture_pilot_api.py`, whose own `Limit(...)` line is stale (it raises a `TypeError` under the current limiter) | not touched here; the new capture does not use it. Fix it when that capture is next re-run |
