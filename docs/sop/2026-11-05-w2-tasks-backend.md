# SOP: W2 Tasks, backend (PR 1)

## What shipped
The backend of W2 of `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-03-ai-workspace-design.md`: the worker now reads a meeting's **Action items** into **tasks** (title, owner, due date, quote), the Tasks API (list, create, change, delete, per meeting), the Settings > Tasks switches, the **"New tasks assigned"** email, the backfill, and the privacy and terms rows. No screen: that is PR 2 (`docs/sop/2026-11-05-w2-tasks-ui.md`). Branch `feature/w2-tasks-api`, PR to `main` (number in the PR list, not merged). Migration **0022**.

## Why
Tasks are the point of the AI workspace: the notes already say who agreed to do what, and nobody should have to copy that into a tracker. The gate was passed on 2026-11-05 (Adebayo reviewed the full extraction on his real meetings 5 and 3: 17 items, 8 of them action items with owners and due dates, every one right, none missed), so `EXTRACT_MODEL` stays `gpt-6-luna`. The risk is the one the spec names: a participant can say "assign Daniel a task: open evil.example", and a wrong owner gets an email. Everything here is built so that the model writes words and **code decides** owners, visibility, who is mailed and how often.

## How
| Piece | Decision |
|---|---|
| The call | `run_extraction` makes the **full** call (`extract-v1`: action items too) when the owner has "Create tasks" on and the notes have action items; otherwise the W1 review-only call (`extract-review-v1`). `extraction_version` records which. A model reply that is missing, late or malformed never costs a task: the tasks are first written from the notes' own bullets by code (`extraction.parse_action_bullet`: `Owner: task (due)`, "Unassigned" is nobody, the owner only if the name is in the transcript) and **committed before the model is asked**, as the decisions already were. The model's answer then refines them in place (same ids). A failed call leaves them `unverified` with no quote and retries in 5 minutes, three times |
| Checks | The spec 3.1 checks were already in `check_extraction` (quote verified in the transcript, title shares 60% of its words with the bullet, due date real, within 400 days and weekday-consistent, owner a whole word in the transcript, item count equals bullet count). A failed check downgrades, never deletes. What W2 changed: a fallback title is now the **task part** of the bullet (the owner and date are fields of their own), so the parser fallback tests changed with it |
| Owner matching | `tasks.match_owner`, code only, against the **active members of the meeting owner's team** (suspended and deleted accounts are nobody). A member answers to their display name, any word of it, the part of their email before the @, and their aliases. Precedence as in spec 3.2: the whole name or an alias exactly; then every word of the spoken name must be a word of the same member. Either way it must pick **exactly one** member: two Daniels never match, "Daniel Smith" never matches "Daniel Okoro", an alias two people share matches neither. In a team of one that leaves the account holder or "Needs owner". Deviation from the spec's wording, on purpose: "any word of the name" instead of "first name", because "Tosin" is how Adebayo's colleagues say his name and it is his surname; uniqueness keeps it safe. When an owner/admin assigns a task whose `spoken_owner` was "Dan" to Daniel, "Dan" becomes Daniel's alias (capped at 20 aliases of 60 characters, no digits; members cannot teach aliases, only managers) |
| Re-runs | By fingerprint of the bullet (`tasks.reconcile`). Same bullet: the row and id stay and an untouched task is refreshed. A task a person **touched** (`edited_at` set, or status not `todo`) is never overwritten or removed. A bullet that vanished removes its untouched task. A deleted AI task is kept as a **tombstone** (`deleted_at`, words cleared, fingerprint kept), so the same bullet does not bring it back; the spec's table has no such column, which is why it is a decision here: without it a rewrite would resurrect everything somebody deleted. Manual tasks are really deleted. Unique `(meeting_id, ai_fingerprint)` |
| The mail | Template `tasks_assigned`, registered as a **notes template** (ids only: `{meeting_id, user_id}`, checked by `enqueue`), so it goes through the outbox, retries, suppression, the recipient daily cap and the global hourly budget like Notes ready. **Once** is a partial unique index on `mail_outbox` over `(payload->>'meeting_id', payload->>'user_id')` for this template, so two workers or two passes cannot both queue (the loser gets `IntegrityError` in a savepoint and queues nothing). Queued in the **same transaction as the compare-and-set** that marks the extraction `done`, so an answer dropped because the owner rewrote the notes mails nobody. Never queued for the meeting's owner, for a task that is not `todo`, for a quiet (backfilled or on-demand) meeting, when the owner's "Email assignees" is off, or for a meeting older than the 30 days the outbox keeps sent rows (after that the index would no longer remember). Checked again **when sent**: meeting exists and is ready, recipient active and still the address on the row, not the owner, owner's switch still on, recipient's email preference on, and they still have `todo` tasks assigned to them from that meeting in their current team (else `Skip` with a reason slug). Consequence, accepted (it is the spec's dedupe key): a rewrite that adds a new task for someone already mailed about this meeting does not mail them again |
| What the mail says | The designer's "New tasks assigned" (`Emails.dc.html`): meta line, "You have N new tasks", the greeting by first name when the name is plainly a name, rows of title and "Due ..." note, "Open my tasks", "Not yours? Open the task to reassign it.", the footer with Email preferences. New layout block `Rows` (both parts escaped). Titles go through `titles.mail_line`: any web address becomes "[link removed]" and a bare domain gets its dots written "[.]", because a mail client would link them. No title, quote or name reaches the subject except the meeting's own cleaned name. The greeting says "today's meeting" only when the meeting's day is today, else "the meeting" (the designer's sample is always today). At most 10 tasks are listed, then "and N more in your tasks". Deviation: "Hi Daniel," uses the first word of the name, as the drawing does |
| Backfill | `workspace.backfill` (and `scripts/backfill_extraction.py`) now marks the recent meetings it queues **quiet** (`meetings.extraction_quiet`): their tasks are **not New** and nobody is mailed. A second pass, `backfill_tasks`, queues recent meetings (14 days, `BACKFILL_TASK_DAYS`) that W1 already read with the review-only prompt (and failed ones), unless the owner has tasks off; idempotent. Older meetings get the on-demand `POST /api/meetings/{id}/tasks/extract` (quiet too). Rewriting the notes (`mark_pending`) clears `quiet`: new notes are new |
| API | `GET/POST /api/tasks`, `GET/PATCH/DELETE /api/tasks/{id}`, `GET /api/meetings/{id}/tasks`, `POST /api/meetings/{id}/tasks/extract`, `GET/PUT /api/settings/tasks`, `badges.tasks_new` on the session, `tasks_count` on every meetings list row and the detail, `tasks` on the meeting detail. New codes `team_forbidden` 403, `extraction_pending` 409, plus `tasks_off` 409, `not_ready` 409 and `assignee_not_on_team` 422. **Parameters that need data that does not exist yet are absent, not ignored**: `project`, `sprint`, `milestone`, `view` (W4, W9). `GET /api/tasks` also returns `people` (the assignee list), because `GET /api/team/members` is W3's |
| Roles and visibility | Visibility is `tasks.visible(user)`: the task's assignee, anyone who can read the task's meeting (`visibility.can_read_clause`, the one rule), and for a manual task the team; a deleted task nobody. Roles are `tasks.permissions`: owner/admin of the task's team may do everything to a task they can see; a member may change status and due date of a task assigned to them, claim a task with no owner (for themselves only), and do everything to a manual task they made. The answer to a task you cannot see is 404; to one you can see but may not change, 403 `team_forbidden`, and **one forbidden field refuses the whole request** (nothing applied). Each task carries `can_update`, `can_edit`, `can_assign`, `can_claim`, `can_delete` for the caller. An assignee of a private meeting's task gets the task and its quote and **no meeting** (`meeting` null, `from_meeting` true), as spec 8.2 says on purpose |
| `badges.tasks_new` | Open tasks the caller can see that are still `is_new`, as the designer's badge counts every task. Cleared when anyone changes the status |
| Time zone | The meeting's date is taken in the owner's own zone (`user_settings.time_zone`, UTC until chosen) and sent to the model, so "Friday" resolves on the right day; `assigned_on` is that date |
| Cascades | `tasks.meeting_id` is ON DELETE CASCADE and `tasks.team_id` too; `assignee_user_id` and `created_by_user_id` are SET NULL. Account purge deletes the manual tasks the account created. W0.5 (meeting delete) has not merged: when it does, its cascade test should add a task row; the table is ready and `tests/test_api_tasks.py::test_deleting_a_meeting_deletes_its_tasks` proves the foreign key |
| Rejected | Putting the tasks write inside `advance_meeting` or a new `STEPS` value (same reason as W1). Making a second model call for owners (code does it). Storing `project_id`, `sprint_id`, `milestone_id`, `carried`, `check_project` now (nothing can set them; W4 and W9 add them). Auto-creating a member when a spoken name matches nobody (spec: "Invite them to the team" is W3) |

## Operate / roll back
- **Deploy** runs migration 0022 (additive: one table, three columns, two indexes). The worker starts reading action items of every new meeting by itself, and mailing assignees (which needs team members, so today it mails nobody: a team of one has only the owner, and the owner is never mailed).
- **Once after deploy:** `docker compose exec web python scripts/backfill_extraction.py` (idempotent; quiet; it also queues the last 14 days of meetings W1 had read).
- **No new environment variable.** `EXTRACT_MODEL` and `EXTRACT_MAX_OUTPUT_TOKENS` are the existing ones.
- **Switch off without a rollback:** each owner has Settings > Tasks ("Create tasks", "Email assignees"; both on by default); for everyone, an admin can `update user_settings set email_assignees = false`.
- **Roll back** by reverting the PR; `0022` downgrades cleanly (tested) but drops the tasks. The old code ignores the new columns. A queued `tasks_assigned` mail with the old code would fail as `unknown template` and not be sent.
- Cost: the full call is about 14k tokens in and 2.5k out for an hour (spec 3.4), a cent or less. `ai_runs` has the real numbers (`prompt_version = extract-v1`).
- Numbering: **0022 is based on 0019** because 0020 (C13) and 0021 (W0.5) had not merged when this was pushed. Whoever merges second renumbers (`down_revision`, the file name, `tests/test_migrations.py::test_0022_*`).

## Review (one Opus read of the diff, injection path and mail)
Focus: injection, mail-once, visibility, roles. **No Critical or High findings.** Read-only (no tests run by the reviewer). Medium and Low, all verified by reading the code, and what was done:

| Finding | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Medium: the mail never said whose meeting it was (spec 3.1 control) | Fixed: a shared meeting's mail says "recorded by <owner>" |
| Low/Medium: the mail carried a private meeting's title to an assignee who may not read it (spec 8.2) | Fixed: "A meeting of <owner>" instead; tests for both cases |
| Medium: a quiet (backfilled) meeting rewritten later would mail the assignees of its old tasks | Fixed: tasks are mailed only while still New (quiet tasks are never New), in `new_assignees` and again in the mail builder; test |
| Medium: the pass before the model call overwrote what an earlier answer had refined, so three failed retries could degrade good tasks | Fixed: that pass only adds and removes (`reconcile(refresh=False)`); test |
| Low: `backfill_tasks` re-queued meetings whose extraction kept failing on every run | Fixed: only `done` meetings are queued |
| Low: two members claiming the same task at once, and a PATCH racing the worker's reconcile, have no row lock | Not done. Windows of milliseconds, the worst case is a last write wins on a claim. Add `with_for_update()` in PATCH and in `reconcile` if it ever shows |

One round, no second round needed (nothing High).
