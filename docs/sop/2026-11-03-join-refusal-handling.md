# SOP: fail fast when Google refuses the bot's join, and retry politely

## What shipped
A Google Meet join that Google refuses now ends with a plain reason, the bot is stopped, and retries are paced: one automatic second bot 4 to 5 minutes after the refusal, and a manual "Try again" no sooner than 5 minutes after any worker-ended join. A bot stuck on a prompt in the meeting (Vexa `needs_help`) fails after 2 minutes with its own reason. Branch `fix/join-refusal-handling`, PR 35. This is the non-calendar part of task C12b in `docs/spike/meet-join-resilience.md` (section 5). Revised after review (see "Review changes").

## Why
On 2026-11-03 Google refused the bot's "Ask to join" (HTTP 403, "You can't join this video call"). Vexa v0.12.28 then sat in a dead lobby for 8 to 10 minutes and the meeting stayed `bot_sent` with no explanation (meetings 8 to 11). Vexa v0.12.29 (PR #1921; production runs it since about 13:00 WAT on 2026-11-03, and one Mac run reached the lobby 3 of 3 times) types the refusal and re-knocks up to 3 times itself. v0.12.29 is now the main path; the v0.12.28 rules are a fallback.

## How
**The real v0.12.29 payload** (read in `spike/vexa` at `31122f9`: `bot/src/orchestrator.ts`, `modules/join/src/googlemeet/refusal.ts`; not run): a refusal is a typed `denial`, so Vexa reports status `failed`, `completion_reason` **`awaiting_admission_rejected`** (the same value a host declining the bot gets) and `data.reason` **starting with `google_meet_join_refused:`**. `app/vexa.py` already reads `data.reason` as `failure_reason`. The marker is therefore recognised at the head of the reason, never in the completion reason.

| Piece | Decision |
|---|---|
| Recognising a refusal (`pipeline._join_problem`, Google Meet only) | A reason that **starts** with `google_meet_join_refused`; or (v0.12.28) a failure text with both "could not locate join button" and `workspace.google.com/products/meet` (meeting 10). A host declining the bot has no marker and still reads "The bot was not let into the meeting" |
| `needs_help` | In Vexa it is also the Gemini consent gate and reCAPTCHA (`admission.ts`), and can return to `active`; so it is **not** a refusal. After 2 minutes (`NEEDS_HELP_GRACE`) with no segments and no recording the join fails with its own reason ("The bot is waiting on a prompt in the meeting, such as a consent or verification screen, and could not continue. Check the meeting for a prompt, then send the bot again."), no automatic second bot (it would hit the same prompt). A bot with segments or a recording id, a Zoom meeting, and a bot that turns `active` or leaves `needs_help` are left alone; `awaiting_admission` (a slow host) is untouched |
| The refusal reason | "Google would not let the bot ask to join this meeting. This sometimes happens; try sending the bot again in a few minutes. If it keeps happening, the host's Meet settings may be blocking guests." (`REASON_JOIN_REFUSED`) |
| Stopping the bot | `_stop_bot` calls `stop_bot` (keyed by platform and meeting code, so it assumes one bot per code at a time). If Vexa errors, nothing is marked and the next poll tries again, up to `STOP_TRIES` (3); after that it gives up so Vexa being down cannot hold the meeting. Before the second bot is sent, `_old_bot_gone` looks the old bot up: terminal means gone; otherwise it stops it again and checks on the next poll, at most 3 checks, then the send goes ahead and Vexa says no itself if the slot is still taken |
| Marker, not wording | When the worker ends a join it stores `completion_reason` = `google_meet_join_refused` or `needs_help` and `terminal_seen_at` = the time (this replaces Vexa's own completion reason on that row). `is_join_failure` and `_paused_for_retry` read the marker; the wording is free to change. No migration |
| Automatic pacing | `REFUSAL_MAX_SENDS = 2`. The first refusal leaves the meeting `bot_sent` and paused; after `REFUSAL_RETRY_GAP` (4 min) plus a 0 to 60 s jitter that is a pure function of meeting id and send number (`refusal_retry_gap`) the worker sends a new bot. While paused it does not ask Vexa about the old bot (except the gone check above). A second refusal fails the meeting. The gap is counted from the refusal, so the retry is never inside Vexa's own re-knocks |
| Manual pacing | For a worker-ended join, `retry_block` is the existing `no_audio` for 5 minutes (`MANUAL_RETRY_GAP`) and `retry_block_message` says how long is left (it blames Google only for a refusal); after that `can_retry` is true and `POST .../retry` sends a new bot (same lock, hours-cap check and `bot_already_active` rule as creating a meeting; row lock so a double click sends one). Manual sends are not capped |
| API | One new field, `join_retry_at` (null unless paused; documented in `docs/openapi.json`, `schema.d.ts` and the FE guide with capture `103`). `refresh_after_seconds` is `30` while a failed join counts down its wait, so pages keep asking. The retry route declares 403 `hours_cap_reached` and 502 `bot_send_failed` |
| Pages | Both pages say that Google refused the first bot and that a second goes in a few minutes (Next shows the time from `join_retry_at`); the Jinja page refreshes while the wait counts down; Next says Google, not Zoom, for a second Google bot |
| Reusing the Zoom machinery | `send_again` is the old `_resend` body, shared; the Zoom retry (up to 3 bots, `MAX_JOIN_ATTEMPTS`) is unchanged and uses the same `join_attempts` counter |

Rejected: failing at the first refusal and resending from `failed` (a failed meeting would flip back to `bot_sent`); a new `retry_block` value for the wait (it would break the Next.js `Record<RetryBlock, string>`); a migration for a "retry at" column (two existing columns carry it).

## Review changes (PR 35, 2026-11-03)
1. `needs_help` no longer reads as a refusal: own reason, 2 minute grace, no auto-retry. 2. A failed `stop_bot` is retried and the second send waits for the old bot to go. 3. The pause and the wait are visible: `join_retry_at`, `refresh_after_seconds` during the wait, Jinja and Next pages, a web test. 4. Fakes rebuilt from the real payload shape; the dead `completion_reason ==` branch is gone. 5. The timing tests poll from the send at `poll_interval_seconds`. 6. 403 and 502 declared on the retry route. 8. The marker is structured. 7 is under Follow-ups.

## Operate / roll back
Tune with the constants at the top of `app/pipeline.py`: `REFUSAL_MAX_SENDS`, `REFUSAL_RETRY_GAP`, `REFUSAL_JITTER_SECONDS`, `MANUAL_RETRY_GAP`, `NEEDS_HELP_GRACE`, `STOP_TRIES`. The `needs_help` grace and the stop counters are in memory, so a restart gives them a fresh count. No migration: roll back by reverting the PR. A meeting paused for its retry when the old code comes back is polled as before and fails with the generic reason.
