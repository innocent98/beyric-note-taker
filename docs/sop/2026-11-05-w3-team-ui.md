# SOP: W3 Team, screens (PR 2 of 2)

## What shipped
The Team screen, the invite dialog, Invited/Resend rows, the invitation link page, teammate filter chips on Tasks, "Invite them to the team" in the task drawer, the "Share with team" switch on the meeting page, alias editing and the team default switch, plus the privacy "Teams" paragraph and the terms pilot rows. Stacked on PR 1 (`feature/w3-team-backend`, backend and migration 0026); branch `feature/w3-team-screens`. PR: see the PR page (not merged). FE guide: `docs/fe-integration-guide-team.md`; SOP of the API: `docs/sop/2026-11-05-w3-team-backend.md`.

## Why
Spec 8.4 and the designer's `isTeam` screen; the Tasks screen (W2) had to leave out the teammate chips and "Invite them to the team" until a team existed.

## How: what is the designer's and what is not
The designer's file (`Beyric Note Taker.dc.html`, whole file read: nav, Tasks screen and drawer, Team screen, invite dialog, share dialog, the Emails file for the mail) draws: the Team screen, the invite dialog (email chips, Role, Cancel, Send invites), the teammate chips, and the drawer's "Invite them to the team". Everything else below is **lead-designed, not in the designer's file** (approved by Adebayo 2026-11-05; each is built only from the file's own components and tokens and **needs the designer's confirmation**; screenshots in `docs/ui/parity/after/`):

| # | Lead-designed piece | Built from | Screenshot |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Share with team" switch on the meeting page (a side card, shown to the meeting's owner when the team has someone else; a "Shared by" row in Details for a teammate) | the Settings switch (`Toggle`) and the side cards | `app-detail-share-*` |
| 1b | Team default "Share new meetings with the team" (owner and admin) | the Settings row (`pl-row`) and switch | bottom of `app-team-*` |
| 2 | Optional "Name" field in the invite dialog (shown for one address; becomes an alias) | the dialog's own field style | `app-team-invite-*` (field appears after one address is typed) |
| 3 | Alias editing: an "Edit names" link under "Matched in meetings as"; one text field, commas | the Review screen's inline field, `btn` | `app-team-aliases-*` |
| 4 | "Add email" beside a non-member participant | **not built**: left for W6 (it is part of the share dialog's recipients) | none |
| 5 | The question before removing someone or leaving (says what happens) | the meeting-delete ask (Decisions "Remove" ask) | `app-team-remove-*` |
| 6 | The invitation link page `/accept-invite` (the mail is drawn; **corrected 2026-11-07, batch 2 PR B: the presentation now follows `Auth.dc.html:171-208`, see `2026-11-07-ui-parity-batch-2b-auth-landing.md`; earlier note: `Auth.dc.html:171-208` also draws a "Join your team" page, so this page differs from the file, see the UI audit**) and the sign-up form filled and locked with a team link | the account screens' frame (`AuthShell`, `AuthHead`) | `invite-new/signin/accept/dead-*`, `signup-team-invite-*` |

Other decisions: the sentence "Owner manages billing" is trimmed to "Owner manages settings and the team" (no billing exists: spec 2 row 1); the Roles card, "How tasks are assigned" card, count line ("N members, M pending") and avatars follow the design; a member sees no Invite button, role selects or switch (the API's flags decide); the remove button is the design's X, but asks first (the design removes at once, but removing unassigns tasks and unshares meetings); an expired invitation shows "Expired" instead of "Invited" (the API's status).

"Invite them to the team" opens the Team page with the dialog open and the spoken name filled in, as the design does (`inviteFromTask` switches screen); the name travels in `sessionStorage`, not the address. A member is told to ask an owner or admin instead of being offered a button that would answer 403. The existing-account accept flow tells the person that their own team will be replaced.
