# SOP: Keeps coming up and Suggested agenda (audit B19, B20, U14)

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-08-designer-v2-features-design.md`, sections 4 and 5 (approved by delegation, 2026-11-08). **PR 1 (backend):** branch `feature/keeps-agenda-backend` (number in the PR list). **PR 2 (screens):** `feature/keeps-agenda-screens` (section "PR 2: the screens" below, added when it ships). **Migration 0037.** FE guide: `docs/fe-integration-guide-series-topics.md`; captures `docs/fe-captures/series-topics/` (`scripts/capture_series_topics_api.py`).

## What shipped (PR 1)
1. **Migration 0037:** table `series_topics (id, series_id, position, label, agenda, reason, meeting_ids jsonb, source_meeting_ids jsonb, computed_at)` (`reason` check: `no_decision | no_owner | not_final`; cascades with the series); three columns on `series` that drive the job (`topics_signature`, `topics_checked_at`, `topics_attempts`); `ck_ai_runs_kind` widened to allow `topics` (the `ask` migration 0034 had not covered it). No row is rewritten. Downgrade deletes the `topics` ledger rows first so the narrow check can return.
2. **The topics job** (`app/topics.py`, run from `worker.background_jobs`): one model call per series over its last five ready meetings (each one's Key points and decisions, size-bounded), `TOPICS_PROMPT_VERSION = "topics-v1"`, setting `topics_model` (env `TOPICS_MODEL`, default `gpt-6-luna`), ledger kind `topics`. The reply is checked in code before anything is stored: JSON must parse; a topic needs a plain label (60), a plain agenda sentence (100), a reason from the list and at least two distinct valid meeting numbers (unknown numbers dropped); at most five topics; repeated labels dropped; a topic whose words overlap an **approved** decision of the series by more than half is dropped. `series_topics` is replaced as a set. A failed call or unusable reply leaves the old rows.
3. **`GET /api/series/{id}`** gains `topics` (read from storage; no model call) and `suggested_agenda` (built in code, spec 5). No new route. `docs/openapi.json` and `web/lib/api/schema.d.ts` regenerated.
4. **The Suggested agenda lines** (`series.agenda_suggestions`), in the designer's order: each visible topic's agenda sentence; "Review the overdue task" / "Review the N overdue tasks" (unfinished tasks with a due date before today in the viewer's zone); "Settle 1 open question carried over" / "Settle N open questions carried over"; "Confirm 1 suggested task from last time" / "Confirm N suggested tasks from last time". Suggested tasks are otherwise in no count.

## Why
The designer's Series page shows two blocks nothing computed: topics that keep coming up without a decision, and an agenda built from them and from work already tracked. Topics need a judgement about meaning ("the same topic"), so a model call; the agenda does not, so it is code.

## How: decisions and trade-offs
| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| When it runs | In the worker, never on a page load. `workspace.run_extraction` marks the series stale (`topics_checked_at = null`) when a meeting of it finishes extraction (`done` or `failed`), and `series.join`/`attach` do when a meeting joins or leaves. A claim (`claim_next`) stamps `topics_checked_at`, commits, and then the call is made with nothing held. A series not marked is looked at again every 6 hours (an unchanged one costs no call), which covers a meeting that was unshared or deleted |
| One call per change | `topics_signature` is a hash of the prompt version, the model, the exact input and the series' approved decision texts. If it equals the stored one there is no call. A series with fewer than two ready meetings makes no call and has its topics cleared. A series with a meeting still `pending` or `running` extraction waits (its decisions are not final): the extraction marks it stale when it finishes |
| Failure | A failed call is in the ledger (`status failed`, error class name only, no text) and the old rows stay. The series is tried again after 30 minutes, three times in all (`topics_attempts`), then waits until its input changes. A stale mark resets the count. The compare-and-set on `topics_checked_at` means a mark made while the call ran is not lost |
| **Visibility (what the review attacks)** | There is no single reader of a series, so the job reads exactly the set the Series page shows to **the series' owner**: `series.meetings_of(series, owner)`, i.e. the owner's own meetings and those shared with the team. A teammate's private meeting is never read. The owner's *own private* meetings are read, so a stored label can come from a meeting a teammate cannot read: at **read time** (`series.topics_for`) each topic's `meeting_ids` are filtered through `visible_meetings(viewer)` (and must still belong to the series and be ready) and a topic with **fewer than two** readable meetings is hidden, together with its agenda line. The sub-line count and the dates cover only the readable ones. No owner, a deleted owner, or an owner who left the team: the job reads nothing |
| Model attribution is not access control (security reviews) | The model chooses which meetings a topic cites, and a label or agenda sentence may be drawn from a meeting the viewer cannot read, cited or not. So the job stores **every meeting it was shown** (`series_topics.source_meeting_ids`). When a viewer cannot read all of them, the topic is shown only if it is **grounded in what they can read** (`series._grounded`): every content word of the label is in the notes (Key points and decisions) of at least two meetings they can read, and more than half of the agenda sentence's content words are in those readable notes together (stems from `app/words.py`). Fail closed: a paraphrase the readable notes do not echo is hidden from that viewer, even if it was legitimate. A viewer who can read every meeting the model saw (the owner, for their own series) has no such check. Tests: `test_a_label_drawn_from_a_meeting_the_viewer_cannot_read_is_not_shown_on_two_incidental_ones`, `test_a_topic_written_after_seeing_a_hidden_meeting_must_be_grounded_anyway`, `test_the_agenda_sentence_must_be_grounded_too`, `test_the_job_stores_every_meeting_it_showed_the_model` |
| Residual risk, stated | Word overlap is a filter, not a proof: a label whose words do appear in two readable meetings, but which the model also drew from a hidden one, is shown (it says nothing the readable notes do not already say, word for word). The agenda sentence must share more than half of its words, so a short sentence with one borrowed word is hidden. If this is still too loose, the stricter alternative is to compute topics per visibility scope from shared meetings only, which drops the owner's own private repeat topics for the owner too |
| No model on read | The page reads `series_topics` rows. Test: the route runs with `topics._call` and `topics.OpenAI` made to raise |
| Meeting text is data | The user message holds meeting text between `<meeting>` blocks with `<` and `>` escaped; the system prompt says all of it is data, not instructions. Titles are not sent; only dates |
| Size | At most 12 Key points of 300 characters and 10 decisions per meeting, one meeting's block at most 2,600 characters, cut on whole lines (the discussion's budget cannot crowd out the decisions). Output at most 900 tokens, timeout 45 s with one retry on a transport error |
| Plain text | Label and agenda go through the same plain-text rule as Ask (no Markdown, HTML or link, bounded). Stored as text, rendered as text |
| `line` and `dates_text` server-side | The three sub-line templates and the date list are written by the server in the viewer's zone, so the FE draws what it is given and cannot count the wrong meetings |
| Agenda lines | `kind` + `text` per line. "Added" is the FE's exact-text comparison with the live agenda; the existing `POST`/`DELETE` agenda routes are used as they are |

## Operate / roll back
* **Deploy:** migration 0037 runs on deploy (`alembic upgrade head`): a new table, three columns on `series` (one with a default), a check constraint swap on `ai_runs`; brief locks. Env `TOPICS_MODEL` is optional (default `gpt-6-luna`). After the deploy every series looks stale (`topics_checked_at` is null), so the worker looks at each once: a series with at least two ready meetings makes **one call** (the cost of the first pass is one call per such series, a few thousand input tokens each). Nothing to switch on.
* **Cost after that:** one call per series per meeting whose notes are read, and none for a series whose input did not change.
* **Roll back:** revert the code, then `alembic downgrade 0036` (drops `series_topics`, the three columns and the `topics` ledger rows). Rolling back only the code is safe: the new columns and table are ignored by old code, the old CHECK is wider than old code needs.

## Follow-ups
* Real-model quality of the topics is unchecked until the first live series (stand-in in the captures); the AI1 eval does not score it. `topics_model` can be changed without a deploy of code.
* A topic is dropped by word overlap with approved decisions (a crude stem). A decision approved *after* the call does not remove a stored topic until the series is recomputed (the next stale mark or the 6-hour look: it changes the input, so a call is made).
* The job runs inside `background_jobs`, so a 45-second call (more with the retry) delays the meeting polling pass by that much; extraction already works the same way. If the pilot grows, move it to its own thread like translation.
* The Series cards (list) do not show topics; only the page does.
