 Last updated on August 12, 2026
 As part of providing our services, we use a number of trusted third-party providers (“sub-processors”) to process data on our behalf. Below is a list of our current sub-processors, their purpose, and the country where data is processed or stored.
 Sub-processor Purpose Location 
 Hetzner Hosting Europe, Germany 
 Linode (Akamai) Hosting Japan (Tokyo) 
 AWS Hosting / Storage / Backups Europe, Ireland; United States & Japan (bot instances, by meeting region) 
 Cloudflare DNS / Load Balancing / Security Global (edge network) 
 Sentry Error-reporting Europe, Germany 
 Stripe Payment Processing Global / Undisclosed 
 Resend Email Service United States 
 Groq Transcription United States 
 Assembly AI Transcription Europe, Ireland & United States (realtime) 
 Deepgram Transcription Europe 
 Speechmatics Transcription Global (Azure) 
 Rev AI Transcription Europe, Germany 
 ElevenLabs Transcription United States 
 Gladia Transcription Europe, France 
 Soniox Transcription United States 
 OpenAI Transcription United States 
 Mistral / Voxtral Transcription Europe, France 
 xAI Transcription United States 
 Google Cloud Speech-to-Text Transcription Europe (EU multi-region) 
 AWS Transcribe Transcription Europe, Germany (Frankfurt) 
 Mailcoach Email Service Europe 
 For non-EU sub-processors, appropriate safeguards (e.g., Standard Contractual Clauses) are in place where required by law.
