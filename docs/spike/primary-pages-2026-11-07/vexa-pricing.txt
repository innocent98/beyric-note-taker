 Frequently asked questions
 What's included in Build on Vexa?
 The base rate covers bot infrastructure: audio capture, webhooks, and 12 months of audio storage. Add real-time transcription for +$0.20/hr, so $0.50/hr all-in.
 Which plan is right for me?
 Individual is for one person and their agents. Build on Vexa is for products built on the API. Self-host from GitHub with no licence fee, and talk to us for Enterprise.
 How does the free credit work?
 Every new account gets $2 in free bot credit—no credit card required. That covers ~4 hours of transcribed meetings at $0.50/hr all-in. Top-ups start at $10; automatic top-ups follow the same minimum. All features are available.
 Can I self-host Vexa?
 Yes. Vexa is Apache 2.0. Deploy it with Docker Compose, Kubernetes or OpenShift on your own servers. Run your own transcription unit and model, and the audio stays inside your network.
 How does Vexa compare to Recall.ai?
 Recall.ai is a hosted API, so its bots run on its infrastructure. Vexa is open source under Apache-2.0 and can run on yours, with the recordings, the transcripts and the model under your control. See our detailed comparison .
 What's the Individual plan for?
 Individual is the seat: $12 per seat per month for one person and the agents they connect over MCP. One concurrent bot, unlimited meetings, the REST API, WebSockets, and the UI Dashboard.
