 Sub-processors 
 Last updated: October 21, 2026
 These are the third parties that process personal data on our behalf when you use the hosted Vexa service. This page is the sub-processor list referenced by our DPA and the recipient disclosure referenced by our Privacy Notice .
 All Vexa-operated infrastructure runs in one region: Seattle, Washington, USA — Akamai Connected Cloud (Linode) region us-sea . We do not currently offer EEA or UK hosting.

 Processors that handle your meeting content — audio, transcripts and meeting metadata.

 Not in use
 We are naming these because earlier versions of this page listed them or left them as placeholders. None of them process any Vexa data today: we operate no transactional email provider , no third-party error-monitoring or crash-reporting service (monitoring is self-hosted Prometheus and Grafana inside our own cluster), and no Umami analytics . Vultr — named as our host on earlier versions of this page — is not a vendor we use and has never held Vexa data.
 Vendors that receive no personal data are not listed above: our container registry (Docker Hub) and the source of our blog articles (GitHub).
 Change procedure
 We will post updates here ≥14 days before adding/replacing a sub-processor (except urgent replacements for security/legal reasons, where we'll notify as soon as practicable). You may object on reasonable grounds; if unresolved, you may terminate affected services.
