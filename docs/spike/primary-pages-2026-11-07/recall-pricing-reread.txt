 Frequently Asked Questions
 Can I try Recall.ai for free?
 Yes! When you sign up on the Pay As You Go plan, your first 5 hours of recording are free.
 Does the number of participants in a meeting affect billing?
 No. It doesn't matter how many participants there are in a meeting. The only thing we charge on is how long the meeting is. 
For example, a 1 hour meeting with 10 participants is billed at the same rate as a 1 hour meeting with 1 participant. 
 How does pricing work with the Meeting Bot API vs Desktop Recording SDK?
 The Meeting Bot API and Desktop Recording SDK are different form factors to record meetings. The price of using them is the same. That means on the Pay As You Go plan, 1 hour of recording with the Meeting Bot API and 1 hour of recording with the Desktop Recording SDK are both $0.50.
 Is pricing prorated if a recording is not a full hour long?
 Yes, Recall.ai prorates billing to the second. For example, if you have a 30 minute meeting on the Pay As You Go plan, you are only charged $0.25, instead of $0.50.
 How do you calculate the length of time a bot is recording?
 https://docs.recall.ai/docs/bot-usage 
 Do you offer volume discounts?
 Yes! We offer volume discounts and custom pricing. If you're interested in learning more about this, please book a call with our sales team. 
 How much is the Calendar API?
 The Calendar API is free to use. There are two separate implementations to support basic and more custom scheduling. Read the docs to learn more about Recall.ai's calendar API.
 Does Recall.ai have a startup program?
 Yes. Early-stage startups with meeting data built into their product can apply for an introductory rate of $0.25 per hour for their first 10,000 hours. Fill out the application form to apply for the startup program .
‍
 Are all bot types priced the same?
 All standard bots incur the same cost. However, if you need 4-core bots for your use case, there is an additional $0.10/hr charge due to the cost of running larger machines and if you need GPU bots there is an additional $1/hr charge. These bots are typically only used for output media use cases in which you plan to have your bot output audio/video into the call. Feel free to reach out to our team for help understanding which bot type is best for your use case.
‍
 How can I get help using the product?
 If you're on the Pay As You Go plan, you can contact our support team by emailing support@recall.ai, using the chat bubble in the dashboard, or using the "Ask AI" feature in our API docs. If you're on the Launch or Enterprise plan, you can contact our team by messaging in our shared Slack channel.
