What Counts as Billable Time
Meeting Bot Billable Time
Billable time =
created_at
to terminal status
Billable scenarios:
Bot joins meeting and records → Full duration
Bot admitted then meeting ends → Full duration including lobby wait
Bot waits 5 minutes, admitted, records 30 minutes → 35 minutes
Free scenarios:
Bot not admitted within 10 minutes → Free (first 10 min grace period)
Bot cancelled before joining → No charge
Bot fails during boot → No charge

Exceeding Limits
Concurrent Bot Limit
If you try to create a bot when your organisation is at its concurrency limit, the API returns HTTP
403
:
Copy
{
"message"
:
"You have reached the limit of concurrent meeting bots for your plan."
}
The request fails immediately: no bot is created or queued, and Skribby does not retry it automatically.
Resolution:
Wait for active bots to finish processing
Upgrade to PAYG+ and negotiate a custom limit above your expected peak
See
Authenticated Bot Capacity
for capacity-planning details
Monthly Hour Limit (Free Plan)
If you exceed 5 hours on the Free plan:
Copy
{
"message"
:
"You have exceeded your monthly bot hours limit."
}
Resolution:
Wait for the next billing cycle
Upgrade to Pay As You Go
