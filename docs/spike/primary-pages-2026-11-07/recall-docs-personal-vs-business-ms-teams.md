# Personal vs Business Microsoft Teams

Difference between personal and business Microsoft teams accounts and meeting URLs

There are 2 distinct versions of Microsoft Teams.

# Business Microsoft Teams

This is also known as "Teams for Work or School". This is the version of Microsoft Teams every company uses. Meeting links will have the form `https://teams.microsoft.com/...`.

You can sign up for a Business Microsoft Teams account [here](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/business/compare-all-microsoft-365-business-products-b).

# Personal Microsoft Teams

This is also known as "Teams for Home or Small Business". Meeting links will have the form `https://teams.live.com/...`.

If you create a Microsoft Teams meeting with your personal Outlook or Microsoft account, the meeting will be considered a Personal Microsoft Teams meeting.
